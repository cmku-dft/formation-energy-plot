Convention

Ef[Dq] = Etot[Dq] − Etot[bulk] − Σi Δni (μiref + Δμi) + q(EVBM + EF) + Ecorr

EF is referenced to the VBM. Ecorr is whatever finite-size correction you supply per entry — FNV, eFNV, Lany–Zunger — the app treats it as a fixed additive term and never recomputes it. Potential alignment should already be folded into that number.
